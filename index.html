MY PROFILE — tap your own photo above. Names change to match your point of view, and the TemPad greets you by name.

SCREEN FIT — adds space on all four sides. Press [+] until the orange border is fully visible, [−] if there is too much black space.
RIGHT EDGE — pulls in only the right side. Use this if the right corners stay cut off while the left has room to spare. Both settings are remembered on this phone.

DISPLAY — the four buttons on the left column set text size. Top two step up and down, the third picks the largest size that still fits, the fourth picks the smallest. Your choice is remembered.
